This deck is based on an Italian Database Systems university course, largely following the book *Database Systems: Concepts, Languages & Architectures* by Paolo Atzeni. The flashcards often include figures and embedded content to facilitate and improve learning. Several exercises are also included (not meant to be memorized), with solutions on the back to test what you have learned.

⚠ **Note:** some figures and visual materials have not been translated from Italian to English and therefore may still contain Italian text.
