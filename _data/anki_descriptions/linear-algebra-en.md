This deck focuses on the basics of Linear Algebra and Combinatorics (discrete mathematics), core subjects in university courses of Computer Science, Mathematics, and Engineering. I created it while attending a Computer Science course in Italy.

It is not just a list of definitions: my goal was to build a set of flashcards that could replace (at least partially) the slow consultation of external textbooks whenever doubts arise during study. The cards combine clear and complete explanations, worked examples, and exercises, with extensive use of MathJax, SVG, and some interactive HTML (iframe) elements. Some cards are longer than a typical Anki flashcard and may not be easy to study at first sight: this slightly controversial choice comes from my need to keep the content as complete as possible, avoid excessively increasing the already large number of cards in the deck, and prevent fragmenting concepts too much.

The deck includes both theory and practice, progressively covering all the fundamental topics: from logic and combinatorics to matrices, vector spaces, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, and diagonalization.

### Deck structure:

```
Logic and sets
Relations and functions
Elements of Combinatorics
Operations, Algebraic Structures, and integers ℤ
Vector spaces 𝕂ⁿ
Matrices
Linear Systems
Linear functions
Eigenvalues and diagonalization
```

🖼️ Features:

* Mathematical formulas typeset with MathJax
* Vector diagrams and illustrations (SVG), no raster images
* Interactive embeds to visualize key geometric concepts
* Examples and exercises with solutions on the back of the card
