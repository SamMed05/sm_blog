I created this deck during my university studies in Algorithms and Data Structures (Computer Science) to have a unified and interactive source of condensed information and resources covering all the main topics taught in the course.

A distinctive feature of this deck is the complete absence of raster images (it weighs less than 5 MB with 500+ flashcards), replaced by clean and editable SVGs, embedded interactive websites and YouTube videos, and advanced MathJax usage.

📕 The content is largely based on the popular book *Introduction to Algorithms (CLRS)*, which is widely considered the standard reference text and university textbook for DSA globally; however, the deck does not cover every single topic present in the book, and the content of the flashcards has been heavily adapted.

While the flashcards may often seem quite long, I found them appropriate given the overall amount of information. They do not consist solely of theoretical concepts, but also integrate various exercises to test theory comprehension and problem-solving skills.<br>
One recommendation is not to memorize definitions and exercises, but rather to understand their meaning and test your comprehension by writing down the answer or algorithm by hand, reasoning through it calmly before revealing the solution on the back. This will take time, but by using this deck I believe you will save much more time than those who have no alternative other than the textbook.<br>

Each flashcard and its embedded figures are designed to automatically support both light and dark themes. For content arranged across multiple columns, a flex layout is used to maintain a responsive design compatible with AnkiDroid (Android) and AnkiMobile (iOS) on smartphones.<br>
The use of vanilla MathJax (no LaTeX) and the absence of custom tags or card types (except for an optional syntax highlighting add-on) maximizes compatibility across any system without requiring any additional setup.
